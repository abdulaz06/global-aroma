# Orders, enquiries and seller notifications

Public review of Global Aroma’s storefront and WooCommerce documentation, 23 November 2026.

## What a visitor can establish

The official shop lists fragrance oils and diffusers with AED prices. The GA-OV-5 page offers **Add to cart** and **Buy now**. An anonymous visit to checkout with an empty basket redirected to the cart. The scent collection also offers product-specific enquiry links. These are distinct customer journeys. Payment methods, successfully processed payments, the seller’s notification recipients and actual inbox delivery remain unverified. This review used public pages and submitted zero orders or messages. [Official shop](https://globalaroma.me/shop/) · [GA-OV-5](https://globalaroma.me/product/ga-ov-5/) · [Checkout](https://globalaroma.me/checkout/) · [Scent collection](https://globalaroma.me/scent-collection/)

The website concept sends product visitors to the official store. Its Studio and contact page prepare an editable email draft. The visitor must send it from their email app. The draft can start a discussion about a project or price. It creates no WooCommerce order record, payment or formal quotation. [Contact implementation](../src/pages/contact.astro)

## Purchasing links in the concept

The **Shop products** navigation opens the shop section on the Aroma division page. Its GA-OV-5 and Arabic Coffee links lead directly to the corresponding official product pages. Both public pages returned HTTP 200 and displayed WooCommerce purchase forms. Arabic Coffee asks the buyer to select a volume. The two category links show additional products. Stock, prices, selection, checkout and the order record stay with the company’s WordPress installation. The review did not add products to a basket or submit a transaction. [GA-OV-5 diffuser](https://globalaroma.me/product/ga-ov-5/) · [Arabic Coffee oil](https://globalaroma.me/product/arabic-coffee/) · [All oils](https://globalaroma.me/product-category/oils/) · [All diffusers](https://globalaroma.me/product-category/diffusers/)

The official scent-box page returned HTTP 200, but its Buy Now link has the placeholder destination `PASTE-BUY-NOW-URL-HERE`. The concept routes scent-box visitors to a prefilled enquiry. A merchant can supply a real orderable SKU if the sampler should be sold online. The all-products shop also shows an unrelated keyboard promotion. The concept prioritizes product links to give owners a representative demonstration. [Scent boxes](https://globalaroma.me/scent-boxes/) · [All-products shop](https://globalaroma.me/shop/)

## How the seller would normally hear about an order

WooCommerce holds the order in **WooCommerce → Orders**. Its standard **New order** email uses the recipient list under **WooCommerce → Settings → Emails → New order**. The default destination is the WordPress administration email and the merchant can change it or disable the message. The store’s published enquiry email and WhatsApp number do not establish who receives order alerts. [WooCommerce email settings](https://woocommerce.com/document/configuring-woocommerce-settings/emails/)

Status matters. Core triggers the seller email on transitions including **Pending payment → Processing** and **Pending payment → On hold**. An order sitting at **Pending payment** can be visible to staff without an alert. An **On hold** order may still await confirmation of an offline payment. A seller alert alone cannot confirm collected funds. Personal inbox delivery also depends on the sending service and the recipient’s mail system. [Core notification triggers](https://github.com/woocommerce/woocommerce/blob/trunk/plugins/woocommerce/includes/emails/class-wc-email-new-order.php) · [Order statuses](https://woocommerce.com/document/managing-orders/order-statuses/) · [Email troubleshooting](https://woocommerce.com/document/email-faq/)

Formal online quotes require a configured quote workflow. WooCommerce documents extensions that create a quote request, notify an assigned recipient and optionally convert an accepted quote into an order. Public evidence does not confirm that Global Aroma uses such an extension, or that a seller receives an automatic WhatsApp or SMS alert. [Quote request workflow](https://woocommerce.com/document/quotes-for-woocommerce-pro/request-quote-form/)

## Minimum access and an end-to-end check

1. **Confirm the intended route with the owner.** For each featured product family, record whether visitors should buy from the official store, request a price, or discuss a project. Link to the corresponding product or enquiry page. Keep price, payment, stock and fulfilment decisions with the merchant.
2. **Ask for an owner-led screen share or redacted screenshots.** Inspect the enabled payment methods, New order enablement and recipients, and any quote or messaging extension settings. Have the owner identify the monitored inbox. A Shop Manager account can edit orders and process refunds. A screen share avoids granting those powers or exposing customer lists. The frontend needs no store login, API key or mailbox access. [WooCommerce roles](https://woocommerce.com/document/roles-capabilities/)
3. **With separate owner authorisation, test in staging.** Use a configured sandbox gateway, fictional customer details and controlled test inboxes. Route outbound mail to those inboxes and isolate fulfilment, stock synchronisation and other live integrations. Submit a representative order. Match its ID across the confirmation screen, the Orders view, the merchant email and the customer message. Verify the payment result separately in the gateway sandbox. For a quote path, submit a sample request and verify its reference, destination and approval or conversion step. [WooCommerce test orders](https://woocommerce.com/document/managing-orders/testing-orders/)
4. **Record the evidence and limits.** Keep the test ID, timestamps, status, masked recipient, and observed delivery. If delivery fails, trace the order transition, email enablement, mail log and receiving inbox. Staging verifies that environment. For evidence of live delivery, the owner can privately reconcile a genuine prior order with its matching received alert and share only a redacted confirmation. [WooCommerce email diagnostics](https://woocommerce.com/document/email-faq/)

The frontend handoff works with public product URLs and needs no WordPress credentials. Editing the store’s payment or mail settings, installing a quote workflow, or adding per-seller assignment, WhatsApp automation or fulfilment integrations would be separate store-side work. Those changes require the owner’s scope and approval.
